# WildCamp

> Free dispersed camping map — find public land campsites across the US. Built by Nick Georgalos. https://wildcamp.nickwontquit.com

## About

WildCamp is a free interactive map for finding dispersed camping spots on public lands in the United States. It shows BLM (Bureau of Land Management) land, National Forest land, and user-submitted campsites with details like road conditions, cell signal, and amenities.

## Features

- Interactive map with public lands overlay (BLM, USFS)
- Community-submitted dispersed camping spots
- Filters by land type, road access, and amenities
- Shareable spot links
- Free to use
